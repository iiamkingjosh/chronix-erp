Mobile Sidebar: Which pattern fits best?

The sidebar is currently a fixed 240px column — it needs a different treatment on mobile. Pick the approach that feels right for a dense ERP like Chronix.

A

Hamburger Drawer (Recommended)

A ☰ menu icon in a top header bar opens a full-height slide-in drawer with the full nav. Content sits underneath. The drawer closes on tap-outside or nav click.
