SUMMARY
-------
A complete, FIRS-compliant double-entry accounting system built directly
into your existing Chronix ERP. No third-party accounting software needed.
Everything lives in your Firestore database and your existing codebase.


================================================================
WEEK 1 — FOUNDATION
================================================================

What gets built:
- New data types added to your codebase:
    * Chart of Accounts
    * Journal Entries (double-entry bookkeeping)
    * Expenses (with approval workflow)
    * Profit & Loss Report structure
    * VAT Return structure

- Chart of Accounts (35 accounts) tailored for Chronix:
    * Assets      : Cash in Bank, Petty Cash, Accounts Receivable,
                    Inventory, Computer Equipment, etc.
    * Liabilities : Accounts Payable, VAT Payable, WHT Payable, PAYE
    * Equity      : Share Capital (Moses 90%, Obadoni 5%, Seyi 5%),
                    Retained Earnings
    * Revenue     : IT Consulting, Network Services, Hardware Sales,
                    Branding, Software Development
    * Expenses    : Salaries, Rent, Internet, Fuel, Marketing,
                    Professional Fees, Utilities, Subscriptions, etc.

- Sequential numbering systems:
    * Journal Entries  : JE260514-001, JE260514-002…
    * Expenses         : EXP260514-001, EXP260514-002…

What you can do after Week 1:
- View all 35 accounts in Firestore
- The system is ready to record transactions


================================================================
WEEK 2 — AUTOMATION
================================================================

What gets built:
- Automatic journal entries when invoices are created:
    Debit  Accounts Receivable  (full invoice amount)
    Credit Revenue Account      (subtotal)
    Credit VAT Payable          (7.5% VAT)

- Automatic journal entries when payments are received:
    Debit  Cash in Bank         (amount received)
    Credit Accounts Receivable  (clears the debt)

- Full Expense Management:
    * Staff submit expense claims
    * CFO/CEO approves or rejects
    * On approval, journal entry is auto-created:
        Debit  Expense Account  (e.g. Rent 6020)
        Debit  VAT Recoverable  (if VAT was paid)
        Credit Cash in Bank     (money paid out)

- Profit & Loss calculator that reads journal entries

What you can do after Week 2:
- Every invoice you create automatically updates your books
- Every payment recorded automatically clears receivables
- Staff can submit expense claims for approval
- You can generate a P&L for any date range


================================================================
WEEK 3 — REPORTING
================================================================

What gets built:
- VAT Return generator (FIRS-compliant):
    * Calculates Output VAT (collected from customers)
    * Calculates Input VAT (paid to suppliers)
    * Shows Net VAT payable to FIRS
    * Shows due date (21st of following month)
    * Can be marked as Filed and Paid

- Balance Sheet generator:
    * Calculates Assets = Liabilities + Equity
    * Shows if your books are balanced
    * Current assets, fixed assets, liabilities, equity

- Printed/Exportable Report Components:
    * Profit & Loss Statement (professional format)
    * VAT Return Report (ready to submit to FIRS)
    * Shows your company details, TIN, RC number

What you can do after Week 3:
- Generate a monthly Profit & Loss statement
- Generate a Balance Sheet at any date
- Generate a VAT return ready to file with FIRS
- See if you owe FIRS money or are owed a refund
